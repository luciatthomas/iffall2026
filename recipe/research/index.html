Endive and Arugula Salad

Ingredients

Salad

  • 1 bag arugula
  • 3 Belgian endives, chopped
  • 1/2 cup dried cherries, finely diced
  • 1 cup shelled pistachios, chopped
  • 3 oz. goat cheese
  • 1/2 cup toasted breadcrumbs
    • 1/4 cup panko
    • 1/4 cup Italian breadcrumbs

Dressing

  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/4 cup white wine vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp. Dijon mustard
  • 1–2 tsp. honey
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Wash and dry lettuce and endives. Slice the endives into thin strips, and chop the pistachios and cherries.
  2. In a pan, toast the breadcrumbs until brown and fragrant.
  3. For the dressing: mix together olive oil, white wine vinegar, mustard, and honey. Season with salt and black pepper.
  4. To assemble the salad: pour half of the dressing into a bowl with the arugula and endives and toss to combine. Add cherries, pistachios, breadcrumbs, and crumbled goat cheese. Toss with the rest of the salad dressing. Enjoy!
